# Alkitab 2.0 — v2.1.0 (build 5)

## What's New (Play Store listing)

Revamped Bible reader with verse highlighting, multi-select, a quick
chapter picker, and an audio player. Redesigned home screen and
dashboards. Fixed bugs where highlights and bookmarks weren't saving
correctly. General UI polish throughout.

## Full release notes

**New in the Bible reader**
- Verses now display as individual cards with numbered badges instead of one long block of text
- Highlight verses in 8 colors
- Long-press to select multiple verses and bookmark, copy, or share them together
- Quick chapter picker — jump to any chapter without scrolling
- Built-in audio player for listening to chapters read aloud
- Choose between Sepia, Light, and Dark reading themes

**Redesigned screens**
- Home screen refreshed with clearer stats and reading history
- Progress and Analytics dashboards redesigned with a reading-streak highlight and clearer stats
- Consistent look and feel across the whole app
- Full English translation throughout the app

**Fixes**
- Verse highlights and bookmarks made from the Bible reader now correctly show up in your Highlights and Bookmarks lists (previously they were saved in a way the rest of the app couldn't see)
- Removed placeholder reading-history data that could appear for new users
- Dashboard statistics now reflect real data instead of placeholder numbers
- Account sync no longer reports success without actually syncing
